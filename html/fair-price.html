Fair Price Index

Know standard local cost ranges for transport, food, and daily essentials across Nepal to travel smart and avoid overpaying.

🚕 Transportation

Taxi Flag-down / Meter Base Rs. 50 - 60
City Taxi Ride (Short Distance) Rs. 300 - 600
Local Bus Ticket (City Route) Rs. 25 - 45

🍲 Food & Dining

Local Dal Bhat Set (Standard) Rs. 200 - 450
Bottled Mineral Water (1L) Rs. 20 - 30
Masala Tea / Chai Cup Rs. 30 - 60

🏔️ Trekking & Permits

TIMS Card (Trekker Permit) Rs. 2,000
National Park Entry Fee Rs. 3,000
Mountain Teahouse Room (Night) Rs. 500 - 1,000
